VendorProfit 0.5.0: simplified UI with four tabs: Lots, Notifications, Auto-buy and Settings. Main view focuses on opportunities, server, quantity, tax-inclusive cost and profit; retainer and cache details are available on hover. Compact numeric fields, name/server search and one Chaos scope switch. Advanced controls, diagnostics and purchase journal are collapsed. Active auto-buy always exposes a stop button. Existing settings and purchasing conditions are preserved. Build, regression tests and standalone ImGui layout previews checked; live in-game UI verification remains outstanding.
